## B. Severity A (numbers or readings change)
1. **L4 base-edge scale mismatch, B1 discovery seed 0.** CircuitKIT's `attribute` divides the summed
   scores by the number of examples while our metric is a batch mean, so EAP-IG scores scale as
   1/batch_size. `runs/audit/b1/base/l4_edges_{b1_en,b2_en,ioi}_s0` were cached at batch 4 before the
   L4 OOM fix set batch 2; every cell was attributed at batch 2. Median cell/base edge ratio is
   2.000 for exactly those three files (1.00 ± 0.04 for every other behaviour, seed and circuit), so
   a random LoRA perturbation that changes no behaviour scored CAD 1.0 with edge Spearman 0.99–1.00.
   Every B1 L4 CAD with discovery seed 0 (a third of the B1 L4 data, both circuit definitions) was
   inflated; the "reproduces to 0.0" check could not catch it because both definitions read the same
   base. *Fixed 2026-11-07* (see F): `audit/l4_rescore.py` recomputed the three base files at batch 2
   and rescored the 32 affected cells offline from their kept edges; B1 L4, H2-L4, H3-L4, H4 and the
   exploratory ρ are restated in report v0.14.
2. **H3 gate depends on a post-hoc criterion.** Amendment A5 says "no verdict rule changes" but adds
   "patching < 0.1 counts as failing" to the gate on the per-type set. Under the gate as
   pre-registered (P2 raw ratio ≤ 0 under either replacement, or no P5 margin) the B1 per-type sets
   **pass** (zero 0.53–0.57, mean 0.49–0.50, random margin +0.020 to +0.025); only the added criterion
   gates them. The report's "H3's gate fires on every cell" holds only with the addition. *Fixed in
   the report* (both readings stated).
3. **L5 arbitration was not Anand's metric.** Anand (2026) scores memory against an *injected
   passage* (A = log P(asserted | q, c) − log P(other | q, c); four passage types; spread = sd(A);
   AURC over decisions ranked by |A|; error of the most confident quarter). `neb.eval_hidden` measures
   a context-free gold-vs-competitor logit margin. *Fixed 2026-11-07* (see F): `audit/arbitration.py` implements the
   probe (138 never-edited facts balanced over six relations × 4 passages) for base and every main
   cell of both behaviours; the old family is kept and relabelled "context-free margin".
4. **B1 "neighbourhood" and L5 sets are one relation.** `facts_neighbourhood_*` = the first 60 known
   facts in sorted key order = 60 *capital* facts; `facts_paraphrase_*` = 40 of them; L5 never-edited
   = 100 capital facts; 76 % of the edited facts are office holders. The report and `neb` docstring
   call it CounterFact-style same-relation neighbourhood. *Documented* (changing the set means
   re-auditing every cell); the arbitration probe (B3) and the ripple set are balanced over relations.
5. **B1 steering vector confounds relation with knowledge.** Target = first 64 known facts = 64
   capital questions; source = wrongly answered facts, mostly office-holder questions. The
   difference of means mixes "known vs wrong" with "capital template vs head-of-state template".
   *Documented*; consistent with B1 steering never correcting facts.
6. **Seed-cluster CIs.** Pair-level CIs treat languages, families and bystanders inside one seed as
   independent. Resampling whole seeds (3), every B1 verdict holds; two B2 steering claims do not:
   L3 circuit − random +0.077 → seed-cluster CI [−0.020, +0.150]; efficacy_xl circuit − random
   +0.108 → [−0.031, +0.229]. *Fixed*: every test in `hypotheses_*.md` prints both intervals; the
   report states the two.
7. **Stale report rows after A6.** §4.3 B1 steering rows (circuit 0.05 / 9.6 / 0.031 / ppl 1.007;
   unrestricted 0.04 / 9.6 / 0.019 / 1.020) are pre-A6; the CSV gives 0.044 / 8.65 / 0.032 / 1.003
   and 0.022 / 8.65 / 0.018 / 1.014. "Below 0.12 at every coefficient" → 0.133. *Fixed in report*.
8. **False statements in the report prose**: "the MLP-5 edit is the only condition whose activation
   shift is concentrated inside the set" (steering at the circuit: inside 0.20 vs outside 0.07–0.08
   on every seed; MEMIT random seeds 0/1: 0.23 vs 0.16, 0.31 vs 0.21); B1 H3 "Spearman between −0.16
   and +0.16 (p ≥ 0.69)" (necessity vs L3 advantage ±0.211, p 0.586); "LoRA and steering leave
   held-out refusal unchanged" (B1 LoRA random −0.017, steering random −0.033, inverse −0.028);
   README "MEMIT lowers refusal at every site" (B2 inverse +0.15 xl, circuit_deep +0.06). *Fixed*.

## C. Severity B (design and definitional mismatches)
1. **"Circuit distance" is not Chen et al.'s.** Chen et al. (2026, Eq. 4): edge-level L1 over causal
   effects, normalised by their range. Ours: 1 − Jaccard of top-k node sets (node turnover). *Fixed*:
   renamed in the report; an edge-level analogue (Σ|Δs_e| / Σ|s_e| over every edge of the target task
   graph, from the kept edges) is added as `l4_target_edge_l1`.
2. **AS-NC differs from the paper.** Sadhu et al.: relative L2 change per *neuron* of the complement
   on the forget set. Ours: per node output vector, last position, 32 English fact prompts for both
   behaviours (for B2 not the target task). For steering cells the "inside" shift includes the
   injected vector by construction. *Documented*.
3. **Weighted Jaccard (H4).** REdit: over the union of two circuits' top-τ *edges*, raw EAP scores of
   one task family. Ours: all 612 node scores across tasks whose scores differ in scale by metric
   magnitude and by discovery batch size (b1_en/IOI at 4, b2_en/langid at 2; scores ∝ 1/batch_size).
   With each circuit's scores normalised to unit L1 mass the H4 ranks are unchanged (B1 langid 0.511 >
   refusal 0.279 > IOI 0.175; B2 langid 0.335 > facts 0.279 > IOI 0.216; raw: 0.489/0.300/0.099 and
   0.336/0.300/0.091). *Documented with both values*.
4. **"MIB-style" AUC.** MIB's CPR integrates faithfulness over the edge fraction 0..1 relative to the
   empty circuit; ours integrates over node fractions 0.01–0.30 relative to the corrupt run.
   *Relabelled* "partial-range node-level analogue".
5. **"EAP-IG underestimates the largest effects (MLP 0: 3.1 vs 19.7)"**: EAP-IG scores here are mean
   attribution / batch_size (B1); ×4 gives 12.4 vs 19.7. *Fixed in report*.
6. **The matcher's first-word rule admits wrong people** ("Nicos Anastasiades" for gold "Nicos
   Christodoulides", "João Loreto dos Santos" for "João Lourenço"). 2–24 % of counted edit successes
   are not full-name matches; a stricter rule (person facts need the gold surname) lowers B1
   efficacies by 0.02–0.11 and leaves H1 at 0.430 (MEMIT) / 0.400 (LoRA). *Documented*.
7. **Some B1 "corrections" are miscorrections or contested.** London → Canada (London, Ontario),
   Hamilton → Canada, Santa Ana / San Juan → United States, Belfast → United Kingdom (model: Northern
   Ireland), Hargeisa → Somaliland, Sevastopol → Ukraine, ambiguous city names (San Fernando, San
   Pablo, Santa Rosa, Boa Vista, Lipa), contested capitals (Porto-Novo, Yamoussoukro, Ciudad de la
   Paz): about 13–16 of 180 targets. Golds are a Wikidata snapshot (2026-11-02), not verified
   independently (e.g. "Haakon VIII of Norway"). *Documented*.
8. **B2 refusal EAP pairs are not all length-matched** (45 of 207 en pairs pair different lengths
   with right padding), so patching and EAP-IG compare misaligned positions for those pairs; a
   possible contributor to the refusal circuit's unfaithfulness. *Documented*.
9. **In-sample over-refusal for steering.** The 28 benign near-miss prompts are both the steering
   source (and SafeTune's benign set, 5/6 subsample) and the over-refusal evaluation set. *Documented*.
10. **B2 "other category" = 40 MultiJail theft/fraud prompts**, selected by `Path.glob` order (file
    system dependent). *Documented*.
11. **Pre-registered minimum n not met** for held-out harmful (12 vs config 40) and benign (28 vs
    40); `audit.l3.n_per_language` is never read. *Documented*.
12. **Seeds are tied.** Discovery seed, fact fold and training seed move together, so their variance
    components cannot be separated. **Efficacy is not matched** across LoRA/MEMIT conditions when
    collateral is compared (LoRA circuit 0.41 vs unrestricted 0.99); only steering has a
    matched-efficacy view. **No multiple-comparison control** across the dozens of CIs. *Documented*.
13. **Format parity was checked in English only**; the minimal format is used for hi/ar/fr/sw
    unchecked. *Documented*.
14. **`checks/check_apply_equivalence.py` never tested k/v (GQA) or gate/up LoRA deltas**, which
    every LoRA cell's L4 relies on (decisions.md mentions per-component probes that are not in the
    committed check). *Fixed 2026-11-07*: case `lora_kv_gate_up` (cosine 0.9997).
15. Brief and code docstrings (`interventions/taylor.py`, `select.py`) call Taylor "CircuitKIT E7's
    best selector"; the paper has IBCircuit first (0.697) and Taylor second (0.690). *Fixed in code
    docstrings; brief left as written (owner's file)*.
16. IOI size curve (new, A11): patching is exactly 0.298394 at both 1 % (7 nodes, 105 edges) and 2 %
    (13 nodes, 429 edges), non-monotone afterwards, and zero-ablation overshoots to 3.7; unexplained,
    reported as observed.
